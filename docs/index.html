Colorado Rail Solutions

Practical Corridor Modernization for Colorado’s Future

Colorado Rail Solutions (CRS) is a rail‑modernization initiative focused on delivering practical, corridor‑based solutions for Colorado communities. Our mission is to evaluate, define, and communicate rail corridor opportunities that improve mobility, strengthen regional connectivity, and support long‑term infrastructure resilience.

CRS develops clear, accessible corridor profiles that help residents, planners, and decision‑makers understand the potential of rail as a modern transportation pathway. Each corridor is treated as a project, and each project delivers solutions.

This site will expand as CRS continues to build out corridor documentation, mission profiles, and modernization concepts across the state.
